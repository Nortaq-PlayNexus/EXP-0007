# Code of Conduct — EXP-0007

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for all contributors regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards of Behavior

### Expected

- Respectful and constructive engagement
- Scientific rigor and intellectual honesty
- Transparent communication of methods and limitations
- Constructive review of scientific claims
- Acknowledgment of uncertainty and error
- Sharing of data, code, and methods openly
- Fair attribution of credit

### Unacceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Fabrication, falsification, or plagiarism of data/code
- Deliberate obfuscation of methods or results
- Personal attacks or hostile behavior
- Unauthorized sharing of private information
- Retaliation against good-faith contributions

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to:

- **Email**: <exp-0007-contacts@example.com>
- **GitHub**: Open a private issue

Reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

- First violation: Private warning
- Second violation: Public warning
- Third violation: Contribution ban
- Severe violations: Immediate ban

## Scientific Integrity

All scientific claims in this repository must be:

- Supported by reproducible evidence
- Transparent about limitations
- Open to falsification
- Correctly attributed
- Free from sensationalism

## Conflict of Interest

Contributors must disclose any financial, professional, or personal interests that could influence their scientific judgments.
